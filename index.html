文言文被砍，
然後呢？ Classical Chinese Was Cut.
Then What?

推薦古文從 30 篇減半為 15 篇，文言比例從 45–65% 降到 35–45%。經典被抽走後，騰出的空間放進了什麼？問題不是白話文，是用什麼標準選白話文。 Recommended classical texts were halved from 30 to 15, and the classical proportion fell from 45–65% to 35–45%. After the classics were removed, what filled the space? The question is not modern Chinese—it is the standard used to select it.

問題在哪 WHAT WENT WRONG

三個後果，不是偶然 Three Consequences, Not Coincidence

01

經典被抽走Canonical texts removed

《岳陽樓記》《廉恥》《勸學》退出推薦名單，古文從 30 篇減半為 15 篇。學生接觸經典的機會被系統性壓縮。 "Yueyang Tower," "Sense of Shame," and "Encouraging Learning" were dropped from the recommended list. Classical texts were halved from 30 to 15. Students' exposure to the canon was systematically reduced.

02

選文標準轉向Selection logic shifted

從「文學經典」轉向「能討論當代議題」。議題正確性可能壓過文學訓練價值，選文邏輯被政治化。 From "literary classics" to "texts that discuss contemporary issues." Issue relevance can overshadow literary training value—and the selection logic becomes politicized.

03

爭議文本進課本Controversial text entered the textbook

高三課本收錄〈泳褲〉，最被記住的是那個詞，不是文字、結構或思想。國文課本不該讓爭議本身變成選文的主要記憶點。 A senior-high textbook included an essay remembered mainly for one explicit word—not for its language, structure, or thought. A Chinese textbook should not let the controversy itself become the main takeaway.

家長可以做的事 WHAT PARENTS CAN DO

與其只罵課綱，不如把國文課拿回來 Instead of Only Criticizing, Take the Class Back

1

讀完爭議全文，再判斷Read the full text before judging

不要只讀新聞標題，也不要只讀被截取的那一句。先讀完全文，再決定它是否適合進入國文課堂。 Do not rely only on headlines or the one quoted sentence. Read the whole essay before deciding whether it belongs in a Chinese classroom.

2

找國文老師談教學意圖Ask the teacher about teaching intent

問老師怎麼處理這篇、有沒有帶學生讀完整篇、討論背後的議題。區分「你不認同的文本」和「你不認同的教育」。 Ask how the text is taught, whether students read the whole piece, and whether the underlying issues are discussed. Distinguish "a text you disagree with" from "an education you disagree with."

3

認真評估自學Seriously evaluate homeschooling

如果你已經不信任這套選文邏輯，把老師請出來，帶小孩回家讀該讀的國文。自學不是逃生門，是重新設計一門課的施工圖。 If you no longer trust the selection logic, bring in a teacher and design the Chinese class yourself. Homeschooling is not an escape door—it is a blueprint for rebuilding the course.
